FlowCut

FlowCut is a personal desktop tool that automatically prepares short vertical videos (Shorts / Reels) and publishes them to the owner's own channels.

What the app does

  • Collects source material and assembles short videos on the owner's computer.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the YouTube channel of the person who signed in, using YouTube API Services.
  • Shows the upload status in a local control panel.

How Google data is used

FlowCut asks for access to your YouTube account only to upload videos to your channel and to read basic information about that channel and those videos. The access token is stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
