Privacy Policy

FlowCut · Last updated: October 5, 2026

FlowCut is a personal desktop tool that prepares short videos and uploads them to the YouTube channel of the person who signs in. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, FlowCut receives an OAuth access token and a refresh token for your YouTube account. With them the app can:

  • upload videos to your YouTube channel and set their title, description and tags;
  • read basic information about your channel and the videos uploaded by the app (for example, upload status and view counts).

FlowCut does not access your Google password, email messages, contacts, files or any other Google data.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the actions listed above at your request. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training of AI models.

3. Storage

The tokens are stored locally on the computer where FlowCut runs. FlowCut has no server and the developer does not collect, receive or store your data.

4. Sharing

FlowCut does not sell, transfer or disclose your data to anyone. Data is exchanged only between your computer and Google servers through YouTube API Services.

5. YouTube API Services

FlowCut uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

FlowCut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke FlowCut's access at any time on the Google Account permissions page. To remove the locally stored tokens, delete the token file in the FlowCut folder on your computer.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
